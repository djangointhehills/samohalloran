A few things to help you get started.

Which ensemble should I choose?

Solo guitar suits intimate dinners and cocktail hours. Catfish brings French swing, Daphne Duet pairs clarinet with guitar, and the trio plays American swing standards. Listen to the performances, or tell me about your event and we can discuss the options.

What events do you play?

I perform across Melbourne for intimate dinners, corporate functions and festivals. Share the occasion, venue and atmosphere you have in mind when you enquire.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Your event date, venue or suburb, and preferred ensemble are a useful starting point. If you know your guest numbers, start and finish times, or have particular music in mind, include those too.

How do I check availability and get a quote?

Use the enquiry builder above or email sam.ohalloran@gmail.com with your event details. We can discuss availability and a quote for your occasion.

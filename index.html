Meet the guitarist

Hi, I’m Sam.

I've been chasing the sound of old strings and worn-out records for as long as I can remember.

Influenced by the greats — Joe Pass, Herb Ellis, Django Reinhardt and Benny Goodman — I play jazz guitar across Melbourne as a soloist and with several ensembles.

Whether it's an intimate dinner, a corporate function or a festival stage, I bring the warmth of classic jazz and swing to every performance.

Planning your event

Let’s find
the right fit.

A few things to help you get started.

Which ensemble should I choose?

Solo guitar suits intimate dinners and cocktail hours. Catfish brings French swing, Daphne Duet pairs clarinet with guitar, and the trio plays American swing standards. Listen to the performances above, or tell me about your event and we can discuss the options.

What events do you play?

I perform across Melbourne for intimate dinners, corporate functions and festivals. Share the occasion, venue and atmosphere you have in mind when you enquire.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Your event date, venue or suburb, and preferred ensemble are a useful starting point. If you know your guest numbers, start and finish times, or have particular music in mind, include those too.

How do I check availability and get a quote?

Use the enquiry builder below or email sam.ohalloran@gmail.com with your event details. We can discuss availability and a quote for your occasion.
